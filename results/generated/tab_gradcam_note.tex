BRISC test images with expert tumor masks; the class rows contain the correctly classified images. Grad-CAM++ (input LayerNorm of the last encoder block, predicted class, single identity view). Pointing game: CAM maximum within 15\,px of the mask; ``Centre'': the same metric for a trivial saliency map peaked at the image centre (isotropic Gaussian). Energy in mask: fraction of the within-head CAM mass inside the tumor mask; enrichment: that fraction divided by the mask's share of the head area (1 = no better than uniform). Energy, enrichment and IoU (CAM $\ge$ 0.5 vs mask) are medians over images; per-image values are released with the code.
