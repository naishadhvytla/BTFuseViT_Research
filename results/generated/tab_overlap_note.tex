Number (\%) of test images with at least one copy or closely related image (e.g. a re-cropped copy or an adjacent slice) in the source named in the row. All image pairs were compared (brain-cropped 64$\times$64 thumbnails, mirrored orientation included); a pair is linked when the normalised cross-correlation is $\ge$ 0.85 and the band-pass (difference-of-Gaussians) correlation is $\ge$ 0.85. Every training image linked to a test image was removed before training; the lower rows show overlap between test sets.
