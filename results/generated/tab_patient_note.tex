``Seen patient'': the figshare patient ID of the test image also occurs in the training or validation data of the full model; ``Unseen'': traced to a patient absent from them; ``Untraced'': not traceable to the figshare release (SARTAJ/Br35H-derived images, for which no patient IDs exist). $p$: Fisher exact test, seen vs unseen. The patient-disjoint model was trained after removing every training/validation image whose figshare patient appears in any test set.
